\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:limits}

The decomposition separates two benchmarks that a headline accuracy figure does not, and applied per sub-corpus it separates one benchmark from itself. On HC3 orthography alone matches content alone on all five partitions. On DAIGT V2 content is stronger by a factor of 7.9. Section~\ref{sec:subgroup} narrows the first of those to one collection convention in the Reddit sub-corpus that is three quarters of the corpus, and removing that convention costs the surface arm ten points. A single collection convention can be cleaned. The diffuse stylistic signal DAIGT V2's surface arm reads has no equivalent single cleaning step in our experiments, and it may not be an artefact at all.

What the measurement does not license is a claim that either corpus is clean. DAIGT V2's surface arm reaches 0.9214 weighted F1, so surface form is substantially informative on both. The reason a low surface score would not have settled the question either is given in~\cite{Feng2019misleading}. The finding is comparative and bounded by the arms we built.

Two results here say more about method than about these corpora. A cleaning experiment reporting no change is uninterpretable without evidence the pipeline ran, and a tokeniser that cannot represent the cue being cleaned makes the null a fact about the model rather than the corpus. Both checks cost one forward pass, and neither is routinely reported.

Several limits bound every number above. The transformers see at most 128 tokens, so the DAIGT V2 transformer results describe classification from an essay's opening, which is why the classical comparison is repeated on that span in Section~\ref{sec:results}. The four deployed checkpoints were run at three seeds and move by 0.0005 to 0.0036 weighted F1, but the grid behind them is single-seed. The content arm is bag-of-words, so word order and syntax lie outside it. The surface set is 47 hand-built features, so $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{S}}$ is an upper bound. Five of the eighteen sub-corpora fall below 200 test rows after rebalancing and are reported as underpowered rather than as nulls. HC3 has one generator collected at one time, and both corpora are English, so the orthographic conventions the surface arm reads are not language-independent.

The measurement itself is the portable part. It needs no new annotation and fits two logistic regressions. It yields one number per corpus, and per sub-corpus, that a headline accuracy cannot express, namely how much of that corpus a model could pass without reading the language. We would encourage reporting it alongside any new detection benchmark, as a hypothesis-only baseline is now reported alongside a natural language inference dataset~\cite{Poliak2018hypothesis}. Three extensions follow directly. The first is to run the decomposition per condition on RAID, M4 and SemEval-2024 Task 8, which already carry the labels it needs. The second is to repeat the transformer comparison at a 512-token budget, so the DAIGT V2 results describe whole essays. The third is to widen both arms, adding word order to the content arm and learned character features to the surface arm, and to test a non-English corpus, where the conventions measured here need not hold.
